# Brainstorm a new product idea

- **Use it when:** you want to build something but have no idea what yet.
- **What you get:** one problem worth exploring, picked by score, ready for `/wow-problem`.
- **Run it:** `/wow-brainstorm something for people who cook for the week on Sundays`
- **Not when:**
  - you already have an idea → `/wow-problem`;
  - the product already exists and you're looking for what to improve → `/wow-product-review`.
- **Reads:** your notes, what you already know about a field.
- **Writes:** `docs/product/brainstorm.md`: the problems, their scores, the one you picked and why.

## Steps
1. **Where to look:** problems you have yourself, problems of people around you, complaints in the reviews of existing apps, work people still do in spreadsheets or on paper.
2. **At least 10 problems, no judging.** Problems, not solutions: "planning meals for the week takes an hour", not "a meal-planning app". Time box: 20–30 minutes.
   - Skip it → you score the first idea against nothing.
3. **Score each from 1 to 5:**
   - how painful it is;
   - how often it happens;
   - whether you can reach those people;
   - whether you can build a first version alone in a few weeks.
   - STOP: you pick one.
4. **Hand it over** to `/wow-problem`, which writes it properly and checks it on people.

## Done when
- [ ] at least 10 problems, written without solutions
- [ ] each one scored
- [ ] one picked, with the reason
