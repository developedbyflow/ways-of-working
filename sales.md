# Sales

- **Use it when:** you sell directly to people or companies: a business customer, a pilot, a client, a paid plan sold in a conversation.
- **Not when:** users sign up and pay on their own → `/wow-growth`.
- **Reads:** `docs/gtm/positioning.md`, `docs/gtm/pricing.md`, the notes in `docs/interviews/`.
- **Writes:** `docs/gtm/pipeline.md` (or a CRM), call notes, the objection log.

## Steps
1. **Who to talk to:** a list of 20–50 best-fit people or companies, and why each one fits.
2. **The first message:**
   - short and personal, about their problem;
   - one ask: a 20-minute call.
   - Skip it → a template sent to everyone, and silence back.
3. **The discovery call:**
   - their situation, the pain, what it costs them;
   - who decides, the budget, the timeline.

   Listen more than you pitch; the interview rules apply.
4. **The demo:** only the part that solves their pain, with their data or their case.
5. **Objections:** write each one down and answer it with proof. An objection you hear three times goes back into the positioning or the product.
6. **Proposal and close:** the price, the scope, the start date, and what success looks like. A pilot gets a clear end date.
   - STOP: you send it (`/wow-comms`). The skill never does.
7. **The pipeline:** every deal has its stage, its next step and a date. Review it every week.
   - Skip it → deals die quietly between follow-ups.

## Done when
- [ ] the list is written, with a reason for each name
- [ ] the messages are sent
- [ ] every call has a note
- [ ] the objection log and the pipeline are current
