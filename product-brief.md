# Product brief

- **Use it when:** you picked an idea (a new product, or a big new area of one) and need one page everyone agrees on before writing requirements.
- **What you get:** one page everyone agrees on, with the four risks and how to test each.
- **Run it:** `/wow-product-brief the meal planner`
- **Not when:** you're writing the requirements themselves → `/wow-prd`.
- **Reads:** `docs/product/ideas.md`, the notes in `docs/interviews/`, the market research in `docs/research/`, `docs/opportunities.md`.
- **Writes:** `docs/product/brief.md`.

```markdown
# <Product> brief
Problem: the job story, and the evidence for it
Who it's for first: the narrowest group that has the problem worst
What they use today, and why it falls short
Our idea, in one paragraph
Why now
How we'll know it works: the number
Risks: value · usability · feasibility · viability, and how we test each
```

## Steps
1. **Fill it from evidence.** Mark every guess as a guess.
   - Skip it → a confident page built on opinions.
2. **The four risks:**
   - **value:** will they want it?
   - **usability:** can they use it?
   - **feasibility:** can we build it?
   - **viability:** does it work for the business (money, legal, reputation)?

   For each, write how you'll test it.
   - Skip it → you test only feasibility, because that's what developers enjoy.
3. **Optional: work backwards.** Write the launch announcement and its FAQ as if the product had already shipped. If the announcement is boring, the product is too.
4. **Optional: a lean canvas** (problem, customer groups, unique value, solution, channels, revenue, costs, key numbers, unfair advantage), when the business model is the open question.
5. **Read by someone else.**
   - STOP: someone outside the idea reads it and tells you what they think it is. If they can't, rewrite it.
6. **Decide:** go, change, or drop.
   - STOP: you decide.

## Done when
- [ ] one page
- [ ] every claim has evidence or is marked as a guess
- [ ] the four risks each have a test
- [ ] the decision is written

## Concepts if you get stuck
- The four product risks
- Working backwards: the press release and FAQ
- Lean canvas
